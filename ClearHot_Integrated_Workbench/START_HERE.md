# Start here — ClearHot integrated prototype

1. Extract the ZIP and open `ClearHot_Local.html` locally in your browser; no account or installation is needed for viewing.
2. Choose your recording, confirm or adjust the decoder, and **Apply decoder & window**. If necessary, suggest a display window.
3. In **Explore and tune**, start with Argos 2. Adjust sliders/numbers and press **Compare tuned vs original and play**. The player shows raw, tuned and the untouched original on matching frames. Pause, seek and restart are in the player.
4. Name and save your favorite preset; export its JSON if you want a backup. To compare it in the blinded still-frame evaluation, check **Include tuned preset**, then **Freeze settings**.
5. For installation, privacy, limitations, experiments and the optional training tools, read **README_INTEGRATED.md** and **RESULTS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md**. To send the same improvement task to two agents, send this whole ZIP and **NEXT_AGENTS_SAME_TASK.md**.

This is a research prototype, not a validated improvement. Your Mac and your real video were not tested here. The learning tool has not been independently run end to end in this build. Keep your earlier working version and original recording as backups. The old files in `provenance/` are references, not the main viewer.
